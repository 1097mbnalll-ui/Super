Delete your data

No account or password needed. Send the request from the same phone number you ordered with, by WhatsApp or phone (+966 55 538 9587). We delete your data within 30 days at most and confirm when done.

Deleted: your name, phone number, address, delivery location and notes on all your orders; your notification device token; your wallet account (if any) after its balance is settled.
Kept: order records with no personal identifiers (items, amounts, dates) and wallet transactions, as required for accounting.
Automatic: personal details on completed or cancelled orders are removed after 90 days even without a request.
